About Me

Hi! I am Xuewen Liu (刘学文), a fourth-year Ph.D. student at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CASIA), advised by Prof. Qingyi Gu. I am currently visiting the HPC-AI Lab, National University of Singapore (NUS), advised by Prof. Yang You, supported by the China Scholarship Council (CSC). Before that, I received my bachelor's degree in Automation from China University of Geosciences (Wuhan) in 2023.

My research focuses on efficient visual generation, with the goal of making high-quality image and video generation more computationally affordable and accessible, from data-center GPUs to mobile devices. I am particularly interested in the following directions:

  • Efficient inference: attention sparsity, feature caching, and efficient sampling for diffusion models.
  • Model compression: quantization, structural pruning, and few-step distillation for diffusion and autoregressive visual generation.
  • On-device visual generation: efficient video generation and practical deployment on resource-constrained hardware.

I welcome research collaborations and discussions. Feel free to reach out at liuxuewen2023@ia.ac.cn.
